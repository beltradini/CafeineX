## Blank gradient backgrounds

The `backgrounds` directory contains the six matching 1080 × 1920 masters. They contain no logo, text, screenshots, photography, or generated product content. Use them behind future Stories, Reel covers, announcements, quotes, or pilot updates.

## Visual rules

- Keep at least 70 px clear on each side, 150 px at the top, and 220 px at the bottom.
- Use one large statement, one supporting line, and one CTA at most.
- Amber means caffeine/intake; violet means cigarette context/timing; green means guidance/privacy.
- Avoid cards, device frames, mockups, dense feature lists, cigarette imagery, and false medical certainty.
- Keep `Personal awareness tool · Not medical advice` on claim-led finished assets.

## Rebuild

Run `build_minimal_stories.py` with the bundled workspace Python. The manifest records dimensions, safe areas, and checksums for every export.
