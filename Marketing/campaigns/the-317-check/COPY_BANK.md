## Reply playbook

**“Is this medical advice?”**

No. CafeineX is a personal-awareness tool. Its estimates and pattern summaries do not diagnose, treat, prevent, or establish the cause of a health outcome.

**“Do I need Apple Health?”**

No. Local tracking works without Apple Health. Caffeine sync and sleep context are optional, separate permissions.

**“Can it tell me whether another coffee is safe?”**

No. CafeineX shows an estimate and timing context; it does not determine whether a dose is safe for an individual. Personal response and health circumstances vary.

**“Why a range?”**

Because caffeine response varies. CafeineX keeps uncertainty visible instead of presenting a single number as biological certainty.

**“How can I help?”**

Join the pilot, use it before a real caffeine choice, and tell us one moment that felt useful, confusing, or wrong.
