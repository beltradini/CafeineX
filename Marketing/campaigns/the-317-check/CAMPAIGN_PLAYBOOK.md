# CafeineX pilot campaign — The 3:17 Check

## Outcome

Recruit iPhone users who want to understand both caffeine and cigarette timing, activate them around real logging moments, and turn their first week into useful product feedback.

**Campaign line:** Before the next cup, check what’s still active.

**Pilot mission:** For seven days, log caffeine and cigarette moments, review the combined timeline, then report one pattern or decision window that felt useful, confusing, or wrong.

This is a personal-awareness campaign. It does not promise better sleep, reduced caffeine or cigarette use, smoking cessation, diagnosis, treatment, or an individualized biological truth.

## The growth idea

Most caffeine content competes for attention with facts and generic “sleep better” advice. CafeineX can own a repeatable moment instead: the pause immediately before an afternoon coffee, energy drink, or pre-workout.

The broad discovery route should repeatedly create this cue:

> It’s 3:17 PM. Before the next cup, check what may still be active.

The exact time is memorable, slightly unexpected, and visually ownable. A second route—**“Caffeine and cigarettes. See the timing together.”**—turns interest into differentiated product understanding. Posts can still be published at the local audience’s likely decision window rather than exactly 3:17 PM.

## Priority audiences

1. **The afternoon-coffee regular** — iPhone user who reaches for another cup between 2:00 and 5:00 PM and wants context without judgment.
2. **The quantified-self iPhone user** — already uses Apple Health, sleep tracking, or TestFlight and values transparent ranges over a single “readiness” score.
3. **The variable-schedule professional** — works, studies, travels, or trains at irregular hours and finds generic cutoff advice too blunt.
4. **The skeptical early adopter** — interested in product honesty, privacy, and testing edge cases; likely to give high-quality pilot feedback.
5. **The cigarette-pattern observer** — wants a private way to count cigarette moments, see time since last, add context, notice coffee pairings, and review sleep-window proximity without judgment.

Do not target people through fear, medical conditions, dependence language, or implied health outcomes.

## Message ladder

| Job | Message | Proof to show | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stop the scroll | Before the next cup, check what’s still active. | Real late-afternoon moment | “Your coffee is ruining your sleep” |
| Explain | CafeineX places an active-caffeine estimate, likely range, and personal cutoff in one view. | Actual Home screen | “Know exactly when caffeine leaves your body” |
| Differentiate | Caffeine and cigarette moments share one searchable timeline with pairings, gaps, context, and sleep-window proximity. | Actual History screen and cigarette intelligence behavior | Absorbed-nicotine estimates or causal conclusions |
| Differentiate | A range, not a magic score. | Visible uncertainty and human language | False precision or medical authority |
| Build trust | On-device first. HealthKit optional. Zero ad trackers. | Privacy screen/site language | Vague “military-grade” claims |
| Convert | Join the private iPhone pilot and pressure-test the decision window for seven days. | Three-step pilot mission | Fake scarcity or invented tester counts |

## Offer and conversion path

**Offer:** early TestFlight access plus a concrete role in shaping the combined caffeine-and-cigarette timeline and its decision windows before launch.

**Primary CTA:** Join the private iPhone pilot.

**Secondary CTA:** Reply with the moment you want CafeineX to make clearer.

**Path:** social post → cafeinex.com pilot section → TestFlight → first caffeine or cigarette log → review the relevant context → voluntary feedback after a real moment.

### Critical launch gate

The current site says the TestFlight link is coming soon and the button is disabled. Do not publish conversion posts or use paid promotion until one of these is live:

- a working public TestFlight invitation; or
- an explicit opt-in waitlist with a reviewed privacy disclosure and owner-approved data handling.

Until then, publish only research and anticipation posts with a reply/save/follow CTA. Never send users to a dead conversion path.

## Pilot activation definition

A recruited tester is **activated** after completing all three:

1. installs the TestFlight build;
2. logs at least one caffeine or cigarette moment;
3. returns to view an active estimate, gap, pairing, context, combined history, or submits one voluntary pilot note.

For a privacy-first pilot without third-party analytics, reconcile:

- Instagram and X native reach, profile visits, replies, saves, and outbound link clicks;
- TestFlight installation, session, and crash data available from Apple;
- voluntary feedback tagged by source question: Instagram, X, friend, or other.

Do not claim an activated-user count from link clicks alone.

## 14-day operating target

Scale the absolute target to actual TestFlight capacity. A useful default for a founder-led pilot is:

- 25 activated testers;
- at least 10 testers completing the seven-day mission;
- at least 8 decision-moment notes;
- at least 3 repeated friction themes or one clearly disproven product assumption.

Quality is the gate. More installs are not automatically better if the cohort does not use the decision window or provide feedback.

## Publishing rhythm

- Publish decision-trigger content between **2:30 and 4:15 PM local time**.
- Publish build-in-public and privacy content between **9:00 and 11:30 AM**.
- Repost the Story link in the decision window on conversion days.
- Respond to substantive pilot questions the same day. Collect objections verbatim for the FAQ and onboarding copy.

## Content pillars

1. **Decision moment** — “Another coffee?”
2. **Honest uncertainty** — “A range, not a magic score.”
3. **Product proof** — real screens and exact feature behavior.
4. **Privacy restraint** — on-device first, optional HealthKit, no ad trackers.
5. **Pilot participation** — what to test, what feedback matters, what changed.
6. **Two habits, one timeline** — coffee pairings, time since last cigarette, context, and sleep-window proximity.

Recommended mix: 25% caffeine decision moment, 25% combined-timeline proof, 15% cigarette context, 15% honest uncertainty, 10% pilot participation, 10% privacy.

## Real-user recruitment loops

### 1. Decision-window loop

Publish at the moment of use, not only at launch time. Ask low-friction questions: “What decides your afternoon coffee?” and “Which cigarette moments tend to pair with coffee, meals, social time, stress, or routine?” Invite thoughtful respondents to the pilot only after they show interest.

### 2. Pilot-note loop

Turn real, permissioned feedback into anonymized product notes:

`A tester hesitated here → we changed this label → here is the new screen.`

Obtain explicit permission before quoting or identifying anyone. Never invent testimonials.

### 3. Founder reply loop

Search X for organic conversations about afternoon coffee, caffeine timing, Apple Health caffeine logging, and TestFlight health apps. Reply with a useful observation first. Mention CafeineX only when directly relevant. Target 5–8 high-quality replies per day, not volume.

### 4. Tester referral loop

At day seven, ask activated testers: `Who is one iPhone user who would value this kind of private timing context?` Provide a share card and the public TestFlight link. Do not reward sharing with health claims or fake exclusivity.

## Experiment plan

Run one variable at a time:

| Test | A | B | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | “Another coffee? Check first.” | “Before the next cup, check what’s still active.” | Outbound clicks per profile visit |
| Proof | Active estimate + cutoff | History + weekly pattern | Activated testers by source |
| CTA | Join the private pilot | Pressure-test CafeineX for 7 days | Installs per outbound click |
| Format | 4:5 product proof | 12–18s decision-moment Reel | Saves/replies and activated testers |

Keep a variation only when it improves activated testers or feedback quality—not reach alone.

## Reporting dashboard

Review after days 3, 7, and 14:

| Stage | Metric | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attention | 3-second Reel hold, impressions | Did the moment feel recognizable? |
| Interest | Saves, replies, profile visits | Did the message create useful curiosity? |
| Intent | Outbound clicks | Was the pilot role clear enough? |
| Conversion | TestFlight installs / outbound clicks | Did the landing path work? |
| Activation | Activated testers / installs | Did users reach the core value quickly? |
| Learning | Decision-moment notes / activated testers | Did the cohort generate evidence? |

Pause acquisition if activation is below 30% after the first 10 attributable installs. Fix onboarding or the first-log experience before sending more traffic.

## Brand and safety rules

- Use `#F29438` amber for intake/energy, `#57DB8A` green for guidance, `#9474FF` violet for timing/context, and coral only for genuine warnings.
- Always use real CafeineX screens for product claims.
- Keep the disclaimer on claim-led assets: `Personal awareness tool. Not medical advice.`
- Say “estimate,” “likely range,” “may,” and “context.”
- Never say “optimize,” “prevent,” “fix your sleep,” “detox,” “safe to consume,” or “know exactly.”
- Do not promote nicotine or depict smoking as aspirational. Use caffeine as the broad discovery route and the combined timeline as the differentiation route.
- Present cigarette tracking as private awareness: counts, timing, gaps, contexts, pairings, and descriptive patterns.
- Never estimate absorbed nicotine, define a safe amount, diagnose dependence, promise cessation, or claim a logged pairing caused a sleep outcome.
- Do not use cigarette-pack imagery, smoke glamour shots, or “reward” language in creative. Use the real app timeline and neutral UI instead.
